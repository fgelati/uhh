## Introduction

After having reviewed conference proceedings, journal articles, books, book sections and presentations in order to establish how the German archival science has addressed research data since 2018, I believe three phases can be identified:  
1. From 2018 to 2020: rapid emergence;  
2. From 2021 to 2024: consolidation and broader engagement;  
3. Since 2025: fruitful maturity.
