## From 2018 to 2020: Rapid Emergence

... several archives have joined a NFDI consortium, for example:
- Landesarchiv Baden-Württemberg the NFDI4Memory;
- Bach-Archiv Leipzig, Stiftung Deutsches Rundfunkarchiv, Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln the NFDI4Culture;
- the German National Library (Deutsche Nationalbibliothek - DNB), a
