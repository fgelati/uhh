## From 2018 to 2020: Rapid Emergence

Several archives joined a consortium, for example:
- Landesarchiv Baden-Württemberg joined as task leader NFDI4Memory;
- Bach-Archiv Leipzig, Stiftung Deutsches Rundfunkarchiv, Deutsches Tanzarchiv Köln successfully applied to NFDI4Culture;
- the German National Library (Deutsche Nationalbibliothek - DNB), a
library which holds a.o. Germany’s biggest collection of web-archives and
which is a key player in digital long-term archiving, entered the consortium
Text+.
