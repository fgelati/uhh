## From 2018 to 2020: Rapid Emergence

In 2018, research data was not discussed in any session of the main archival conference (Deutscher Archivtag). One year later, one presentation was held about prosopographical databases, contemporary history and GDPR-compliance.  

Between 2018 and 2020, the National Research Data Infrastructure (Nationale Forschungsdateninfrastruktur - NFDI) was established. Some numbers:
- it encompasses of up to 30 discipline-specific consortia;
- it relies on an envelope of 90 million EUR per year;
- it is financed until 2038. 

In Feb. 2020, a thematic issue of *Archivar* is devoted to research data archiving, making the topic accessible for many archivists. It also presents NFDI.
