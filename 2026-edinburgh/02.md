## Research Data Management

Research data management is the process of transforming scientific evidence (i.e., data) into machine-processable, FAIR research outcome.  
RDM relies both on data stewardship (in order to ensure data quality) and on digital preservation (so that data remains readable as long as it retains value).

See also: DCC Digital Curation Glossary, entry “Research Data Management”, https://www.dcc.ac.uk/about/digital-curation/glossary.
