## Research Data Management

Research data management is the process of transforming scientific evidence (i.e., data) into a machine-processable, FAIR research outcome.  
RDM relies both on __data stewardship__ (in order to ensure data quality) and on __digital preservation__ (so that data remains readable as long as it retains value). It complies with __Open Science__ and __TRUST__ principles.

See also: DCC Digital Curation Glossary, entry “Research Data Management”, https://www.dcc.ac.uk/about/digital-curation/glossary.
